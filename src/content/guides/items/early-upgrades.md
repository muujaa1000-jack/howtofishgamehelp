## Quick steps

1. Choose one early melee weapon rather than funding several at once.
2. Improve its damage at the lighthouse anvil before summoning Spider Crab.
3. Reserve money for the beer quest, basic fishing, and required navigation items.
4. Complete the first boss and secure boat keys plus radar access.
5. On the forest island, prioritize the shotgun over a chain of marginal side purchases.
6. Buy inventory, attachments, and optional mini-boss lures only when they solve a current need.

## Upgrade by gate, not by shop order

The two walkthroughs differ on whether a knife or brass knuckles feels best at the lighthouse, which is a useful warning against a fake universal ranking. Both support the same principle: improve one melee choice enough for the first boss. Splitting money between several weak tools delays the only upgrade that changes progression.

On Island 2, the evidence becomes clearer. The Destructoid route treats the shotgun as the first high-value gun and continues using it on later creatures. That makes it a better progression purchase than buying every lower-cost option simply because it unlocked first.

## Why it may not work

- **A recommended price is different:** launch patches and region displays can change values; compare relative utility, not an old number.
- **Your melee weapon still struggles:** use the anvil and fight Spider Crab through its stun window rather than trading hits.
- **You cannot afford the next quest item:** stop optional lure and attachment purchases until the story gate is funded.
- **The shotgun feels wrong for the bird:** it is a strong early gun, not the correct range for every boss. Switch to an Uzi or accurate longer-range option later.
- **Boat speed is low:** engine upgrades matter, but first confirm the current island quest is complete.

## Solo and co-op notes

Solo players gain more from dependable healing and one well-upgraded weapon than a wide inventory of weak choices. Co-op teams can specialize, but each player should retain one self-sufficient damage option; shared progression does not make an unarmed teammate useful during a boss failure.
