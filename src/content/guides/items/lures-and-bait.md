## Quick steps

1. Read the current quest text before opening the island shop.
2. Use normal lure tiers for routine catches and economy.
3. Treat purchasable mini-boss lures as optional unless an achievement requires them.
4. Complete the local NPC request to receive story-boss bait.
5. Prepare weapons, healing, terrain, and inventory space before equipping it.
6. After the boss, preserve the distinct drop and finish the return hand-in.
7. For a fish already killed once, check Fishipedia for its bait name before buying or casting again.

## The three lure categories

**Normal lures** expand the regular creature pool as you move through islands. They support selling, cooking, and collection. **Mini-boss lures** are bought and can summon optional tougher targets; the Beginner Boss Lure documented on Island 2 is an example. **Story bait** comes from quest progress and points to a specific required encounter.

The story chain demonstrates the difference. Beer becomes the first boss trigger. Ground leeches are converted by the forest NPC. An endangered catch earns a Carrot from the tourist. The [Professional Boss Lure for the Tuna boss](/bosses/tuna/) produces the body that becomes the bird bait. At the volcano, the scientist provides the whale trigger after his request.

## New islet bait and Fishipedia clues

The [Islet Update 1.1.0](https://steamcommunity.com/games/4001890/announcements/detail/717916455762395911) added a new bait alongside mini islands and creatures. [Patch 1.1.2](https://steamcommunity.com/games/4001890/announcements/detail/706658093186943189) increased that bait's spawns on the new mini island from two to three and fixed Tench not spawning from it. Those notes do not identify every catch or publish a full location map; use the [radar's mini-island dots](/items/radar-guide/) to look for the added locations.

[Patch 1.1.3](https://steamcommunity.com/games/4001890/announcements/detail/706658093186943213) adds a bait-name clue in Fishipedia after you have killed a fish once. Open its entry when repeating a known catch or searching for a Drip variant. For a fish you have never killed, the announcement does not promise an advance clue, so continue using the current quest text and in-game item descriptions.

## Why it may not work

- **A normal creature bites instead of the boss:** verify the exact quest bait is equipped on the rod.
- **A shop lure starts the wrong fight:** optional mini-boss lures do not replace story rewards.
- **The quest giver offers nothing:** start the objective and satisfy its counter or hand-in first.
- **The bait disappears after failure:** preserve resources by preparing before the cast; no universal recovery rule is verified.
- **A lure name differs after a patch:** follow the current objective and item description rather than an old price list.
- **Fishipedia does not show a bait name yet:** the 1.1.3 clue appears only after that fish's first kill.

## Solo and co-op notes

Solo players should never use limited story bait without a complete loadout. In co-op, announce the cast so everyone is nearby, healed, and carrying the intended weapon. One player should keep an empty slot for the trophy.
