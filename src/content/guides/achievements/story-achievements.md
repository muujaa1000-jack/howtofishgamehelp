## Quick steps

1. Return Spider Crab’s result to the lighthouse keeper for Who stole my beer.
2. Finish the forest lady’s requested dinner chain after Giant Piranha.
3. Start the desert grill and complete the tourist’s vacation request.
4. Defend the rocky islanders from the large bird and finish the hand-in.
5. Complete the scientist or military whale objective at the volcano.
6. Return the final trophy, receive the RHIB key, and start the RHIB.

## Follow the official wording

Steam gives useful, stable descriptions without prescribing every combat detail. Who stole my beer requires finding the culprit and bringing it to the keeper. Dinnertime asks for the forest lady’s dinner. Vacation asks you to help the tourist swim. Terrorizing bird and Deadliest catch both name the group you are helping. We are so back says to finish the game.

That wording explains why an achievement may not trigger at the exact boss death. A body, trophy, dinner, or final key still has to reach the person named in the condition. Use the walkthrough for route planning, but use Steam’s text as the completion boundary.

## Why it may not work

- **Who stole my beer stays locked:** return the unique Spider Crab result to the keeper.
- **Dinnertime does not trigger:** complete the forest NPC’s requested hand-in, not a generic sale.
- **Vacation is missing:** satisfy the tourist’s endangered-catch route and finish his dialogue.
- **Terrorizing bird remains locked:** ordinary seagull kills do not replace the island quest encounter.
- **We are so back is absent:** return the last drop, take the RHIB key, and start the RHIB.

## Solo and co-op notes

These conditions can be reached solo. In co-op, personal unlock timing may depend on participation and who performs the final interaction. Keep the party together for hand-ins and check each player’s Steam list afterward. If one person is missing a quest achievement, replay the relevant interaction before attempting unrelated fixes.
