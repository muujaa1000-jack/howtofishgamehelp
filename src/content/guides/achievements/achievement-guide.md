## Quick steps

1. Finish the story once and let its quest achievements unlock naturally.
2. Collect simple interaction goals such as grilling, upgrades, attachments, and seagull events along the way.
3. Keep a list of creatures and Drip variants by island for Collector and Fishipedia.
4. Return after the ending for fast boss kills, stunt shots, economy goals, and gambling outcomes.
5. Run Handyman separately with a bare-hands final-boss plan.
6. Run Bean separately with a route focused only on finishing within one hour.

## Group the 28 goals

The official list mixes five kinds of achievement. Story goals include Who stole my beer, Dinnertime, Vacation, Terrorizing bird, Deadliest catch, and We are so back. Combat or physics goals include Noob, Impressive, 360 no scope, Easy, Let me go, Everyone’s dream, and I’m the bird now. Economy and equipment cover engine upgrades, all attachments, roulette, a legendary slot result, and a very high-value sale.

The two collection checks are Collector for every creature and Fishipedia for every Drip creature. The dedicated challenge runs are Handyman and Bean. Treat percentages on Steam as live statistics, not stable difficulty ratings; they change as more people play.

## Why it may not work

- **A story achievement stays locked:** finish the NPC hand-in, not only the boss kill.
- **A multiplier goal fails:** the condition must be true when the creature dies.
- **Handyman fails:** using any weapon during the final boss attempt invalidates the plain-language requirement.
- **Collector stalls:** “all creatures” is broader than story bosses. Revisit each area and separate normal species from Drip variants.
- **An achievement was bugged in an old guide:** patch 1.0.4 and 1.0.5 changed achievement behavior, so update first.

## Solo and co-op notes

Most official conditions do not require co-op. A party can make story combat and collection sweeps faster, but personal trigger ownership can be confusing. If a condition is tied to your action—being picked up, landing a stunt kill, or using bare hands—perform it yourself and check Steam afterward.
