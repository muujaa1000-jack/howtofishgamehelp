## Quick steps

1. Finish the story normally and preserve the completed save for travel and cleanup.
2. Build one island-by-island list for normal creatures and another for Drip variants.
3. Use endgame gear against an early boss for the ten-second Easy condition.
4. Fund and complete the high-value sale or equipment goals on the cleanup save.
5. Start a dedicated Handyman attempt with no weapon use in the final fight.
6. Start a separate Bean route and remove every optional detour from the one-hour path.

## Why these goals conflict

Steam’s official conditions are concise. Collector requires every creature; Fishipedia requires every Drip creature. Those are coverage problems, best solved with a ledger. Easy requires a boss within ten seconds, which benefits from endgame damage against a familiar early target. Handyman removes weapons from the final boss. Bean imposes a one-hour completion limit.

A completionist run naturally explores and farms, while a speed run rejects both. A bare-hands finale naturally takes longer than an optimized rifle kill. Keeping separate attempts prevents one condition from silently invalidating the plan for another.

## Why it may not work

- **Collector is still locked:** a story clear does not prove every normal species was found and killed.
- **Fishipedia stalls after all visible variants:** patch 1.0.5 fixed a prior trigger that required an extra Drip; update before repeating the collection.
- **Easy misses:** the final blow must arrive inside the official ten-second condition; use a weak early boss and endgame gear.
- **Handyman fails:** do not use a weapon during the final boss attempt, even for a convenient opening hit.
- **Bean misses:** cut upgrades, gambling, collection, and optional mini-bosses from the route.

## Solo and co-op notes

Personal action requirements are simplest solo. Co-op can accelerate collection sweeps, but verify that your account receives credit for the relevant kill or find. For Handyman and Bean, extra players add coordination risk; only use a party with a rehearsed plan.

## Difficulty boundary for challenge attempts

Patch 1.0.9 changes creature health and damage but does not state whether difficulty changes achievement eligibility. Choose a mode knowingly, record it when diagnosing a failed unlock, and do not promise that Easy enables or Hard rewards a condition. The official achievement text remains the authority for the named action.
