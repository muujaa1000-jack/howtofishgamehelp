## Quick steps

1. Complete the scientist’s five-catch request and receive whale bait.
2. Stock cooked fish and prepare an assault rifle with useful attachments.
3. Defeat the regular whale while preserving most of your healing.
4. Carry that whale to the volcano and throw it into the intended opening.
5. Keep moving during the mutated whale’s rolls, stomps, lava, and meteor attacks.
6. Return its final drop, take the RHIB key, and start the RHIB.

## Treat the finale as one long resource check

The regular whale is the entry cost, not the ending. It charges and can launch upward before a landing attack; move immediately when it leaves the ground. Once defeated, the body becomes the trigger for the mutated form. If you arrive at that second fight with an empty inventory or an unprepared weapon, there is no convenient preparation break.

The mutated whale adds area denial. Lava pools and falling attacks matter as much as direct contact, especially in the more aggressive later phase. Keep an escape lane between you and safe ground, fire during predictable gaps, and heal promptly rather than waiting for critical health.

## Why it may not work

- **The second fight does not start:** the regular whale must be carried to and thrown into the volcano at the intended route.
- **You cannot sustain damage:** use an upgraded, attached assault rifle rather than an early short-range weapon.
- **Healing runs out:** prepare for both fights and stage extra cooked fish before activating the bait.
- **You die while aiming:** movement has priority whenever a stomp, meteor, or lava zone threatens your position.
- **The game does not finish:** return the drop to the scientist and start the RHIB; the boss kill is not the final interaction.

## Solo and co-op strategy

Solo players should keep a wide loop around the arena and interrupt shooting whenever ground hazards close that loop. In co-op, spread so one area attack cannot knock down the entire party. Rotate revives: one player helps while the others keep the boss moving away.

## Difficulty and save-safety note

Patch 1.0.9 makes creature health and damage mode-dependent. Prepare healing for your selected difficulty, then preserve the final drop and return it before experimenting with settings. If the save crashes while loading or when equipping a weapon, stop repeating the finale and use the [save and weapon crash guide](/fixes/save-file-corrupted-or-weapon-crash/).

## Patch 1.0.12: explosives and the tail

The [official note](https://steamcommunity.com/games/4001890/announcements/detail/698774889153168486) says the final boss should now receive explosive damage even when the explosive lands on its tail. If a hit still fails to register, note where it landed and your game version. Older footage may show different behavior.

For Handyman, older player guides describe gun damage followed by a killing punch. Whether that method works in 1.0.12 is unconfirmed. To try it, stop shooting before the boss dies, let any remaining explosive or other ongoing damage end, confirm bare fists, then attempt the final hit during an opening. Co-op credit and brass-knuckle eligibility remain unknown; check your own unlock afterward. See the [Handyman preparation and steps](/achievements/hardest-achievements/#handyman-official-condition-and-community-final-hit-method).
