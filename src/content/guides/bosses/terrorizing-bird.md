## Quick steps

1. Speak to the island NPC and activate the large-bird request.
2. Follow the [Tuna mini-boss guide](/bosses/tuna/) to catch it with the Professional Boss Lure and preserve the body.
3. Place the Tuna body on the ground near reliable building cover.
4. Move under the roof as soon as the aerial encounter begins.
5. Track the bird, wait for an attack to finish, then step out and fire.
6. Collect the unique trophy and complete the NPC hand-in.

## Make the building part of your loadout

The terrorizing bird is a range and visibility check. The launch walkthrough recommends an Uzi or sniper-style weapon and notes that a shotgun wastes many shots against a distant flying target. The focused boss evidence adds that solid walls and a roof can interrupt the dive and ranged pressure.

Choose cover before placing the Tuna. From safety, follow the target marker or sound cue and resist the urge to chase across the island. When an attack spends itself on cover, expose briefly, fire a controlled burst or precise shot, then return before the next approach.

## Why it may not work

- **The bird never appears:** the defeated Tuna must be placed on the ground as bait.
- **Tuna does not spawn:** activate the quest and equip the correct Professional Boss Lure.
- **You take repeated open-field hits:** move under a roof; distance alone does not stop the aerial attack.
- **The target stays out of shotgun range:** switch to a weapon designed for sustained or accurate ranged fire.
- **The volcano remains locked:** retrieve the bird’s distinct trophy and return it to the quest NPC.

## Solo and co-op strategy

Solo players should use one building and repeat a patient peek-and-fire rhythm. In co-op, spread around different sides of the same cover so the bird cannot pressure every firing angle at once. Call each pass, reload inside, and avoid reviving a teammate in open ground.

## Difficulty note for 1.0.9

Patch 1.0.9 changes creature health and damage through the selected mode. It does not announce a different Tuna trigger or hand-in. Treat older combat pacing as Normal-context guidance, avoid invented health thresholds, and use [difficulty settings](/guides/difficulty-settings/) when the aerial encounter feels out of step with an older walkthrough.

## What to do next

Finish the hand-in and travel to [volcano endgame progression](/islands/volcano-endgame/). Prepare the final loadout with [weapon progression](/items/weapon-progression/) before starting the whale chain.
