## Quick steps

1. Finish the keeper’s beer request and receive the Empty Beer Can.
2. Improve one early melee weapon at the lighthouse anvil.
3. Clear nearby clutter and equip the can as your fishing bait.
4. Reel in Spider Crab and wait for a committed charge.
5. Move sideways, turn back quickly, and attack while it is stunned.
6. Collect the drop and complete the lighthouse hand-in.

## Read the charge instead of trading hits

The two gameplay sources agree on the reliable opening-boss pattern: a missed charge produces a short stun. That is your damage window. Face the crab in open space, keep a medium distance, and move across its line only after it commits. Two or three controlled melee hits are safer than staying for an extra swing and being caught as it recovers.

Do not kite across the entire island. The walkthrough warns that distance can encourage a jump and consume the encounter’s pressure window. The white bar under a story boss is part of the escape constraint, so movement should create a dodge angle, not abandon the fight.

## Why it may not work

- **The crab never bites:** normal lures do not replace the returned Empty Beer Can.
- **It turns before you can hit:** sidestep later, after the charge direction is committed.
- **Your damage is too low:** improve one melee weapon before summoning rather than relying on bare hands.
- **The boss escapes:** stay engaged and avoid long-distance circling.
- **The keeper does not reward you:** pick up the unique boss result and hand it back; a kill alone is incomplete.

## Solo and co-op strategy

Solo players should prioritize clean cycles—bait, sidestep, two or three hits, reset. In co-op, one player can stand forward to draw the charge while teammates stay off the line and attack the rear during the stun. Do not surround so tightly that the charge direction becomes hard to read.

## Difficulty note for 1.0.9

The charge windows and quest sequence here remain source-based, while creature health and damage now depend on Easy, Normal, or Hard. ## What to do next

Return the drop and follow [how to unlock the next island](/guides/unlock-next-island/). The next story fight is [Giant Piranha](/bosses/giant-piranha/), which does not offer the same self-stun rhythm.
