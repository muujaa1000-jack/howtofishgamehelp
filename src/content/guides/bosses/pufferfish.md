## Direct answer

Complete the desert tourist’s endangered-catch request and keep the Carrot reward. Unlock the grill, prepare cooked healing, choose open terrain, and equip the Carrot only when ready. During the fight, leave the line of each rolling approach, attack in the gap, and widen your path when the purple trail appears. After the kill, collect Pufferfish’s distinct drop and return it to the tourist; the hand-in, not the kill alone, advances the route.

## Applies to

The encounter sequence and movement advice come from two independent launch guides reviewed through August 25, 2026. The page retains version 1.0.5 as its route evidence label. Official patch 1.0.9 later added Easy, Normal, and Hard settings, so old expectations about damage taken or fight length are patch-sensitive. This is a source-based guide, not an independently playtested boss report, and it does not claim exact health, damage, or timing values.

## Quick steps

1. Speak to the desert tourist and satisfy the endangered-catch request.
2. Keep the Carrot reward; do not treat it as ordinary bait.
3. Unlock the grill and prepare several cooked healing items.
4. Equip a ranged option you can use while repositioning.
5. Move to open ground away from trees and props before using the Carrot.
6. Sidestep the rolling lane, turn, and attack during the safe gap.
7. When a purple trail appears, circle around its end rather than crossing it.
8. Collect the boss drop and return it to the tourist for progression.

## Why spacing works

The focused encounter source describes Pufferfish as a rolling boss that becomes larger and leaves a purple poison trail later in the fight. Those mechanics make position more important than rushing damage. Backpedaling keeps the player on the same line as an incoming roll. A deliberate sideways move leaves that line. Trees, props, and the edge of the usable area reduce the space available for that move, which is why the arena should be chosen before the trigger is consumed.

The same logic applies to attacking. A long burst that delays movement is less useful than a short, repeatable cycle: identify the roll, leave its lane, face the boss after it passes, deal controlled damage, then reset. When the trail begins to partition the arena, the shortest route is not always the safest route. Going around the end preserves health and open space.

## Detailed preparation and fight plan

First finish the local request. The Carrot is presented by the walkthrough sources as the boss bait, so a Carrot-shaped expectation from another item is not a substitute. Before equipping it, use the grill and make healing available. This guide does not prescribe a minimum quantity because the sources do not establish a universal number and difficulty now changes incoming pressure.

Choose a ranged weapon that does not tempt you to stand still. Walk to a broad area and look for obstacles behind and beside you. If the fight begins among coconut trees, move the battle toward clearer ground rather than trying to thread every dodge through the same gap.

For each roll, imagine a straight lane from Pufferfish through your current position. Move across that lane, not backward along it. Turn only after clearing the approach. Take a measured attack window, then look at the next line. In the later phase, track the purple trail as persistent terrain. Do not step through it merely to maintain damage. As the boss appears larger, expand the distance used to judge the same maneuver.

When health drops, create separation before eating. Healing while the next roll is already aligned can trade the recovered health for another hit. After the fight, slow down. Find the distinct drop, identify which player took it in co-op, and return it to the tourist. New coordinates depend on completing that state.

## Common mistakes

- Equipping the Carrot before inventory, healing, and terrain are ready.
- Fighting between trees, where a correct sideways input still meets an obstacle.
- Backpedaling directly along the boss’s approach line.
- Chasing damage through the purple trail instead of resetting around its edge.
- Healing as an attack begins rather than after space has been created.
- Leaving after the kill without collecting and returning the unique drop.
- Copying an old fight-time estimate without accounting for 1.0.9 difficulty.

## Why it may not work

If the Carrot is missing, return to the tourist and check that the requested catch condition was accepted. If the boss does not trigger, verify the quest is active and the Carrot reward is the equipped item. If every dodge still connects, move to a more open part of the island and start the lateral movement earlier. If progression stops after victory, check all co-op inventories for the trophy and repeat the tourist conversation before attempting another summon.

## Safe recovery

Do not discard a save or unique item because coordinates fail to appear immediately. Preserve the current state, check inventory, return to the tourist, and restart only after noting the active objective. In co-op, keep the player holding the drop in the lobby until the hand-in completes. If the save will not load or equipping a weapon causes a crash, stop the boss loop and follow the [save and weapon crash guide](/fixes/save-file-corrupted-or-weapon-crash/); repeated forced attempts can make diagnosis harder.

## Solo and co-op differences

Solo play keeps the boss’s attention and trophy custody with one person, so movement is predictable but recovery support is absent. Prepare before consuming the trigger. In co-op, spread into a shallow arc rather than stacking on one line. The targeted player creates the roll lane while others retain safer firing angles. Do not revive inside the purple trail. Choose one player to collect the drop, and confirm the hand-in before anyone leaves.

## Patch history and limitations

Patch 1.0.9 officially defines Easy as creatures having 25 percent less health and dealing 50 percent less damage, Normal as the earlier balance, and Hard as creatures having 25 percent more health and dealing 25 percent more damage. This page uses those numbers only because the developer published them. It does not infer Pufferfish’s base health, exact attacks, or a best difficulty. The patch announcement does not state that the Carrot quest or trophy hand-in changed.

## FAQ

### Where does the Carrot come from?

The cited progression sources place it after the desert tourist’s endangered-catch request. Complete that hand-in before searching for a boss trigger elsewhere.

### Should I fight in melee range?

This route recommends a controllable ranged option because the rolling lane and poison trail reward spacing. It does not claim that other weapons cannot work.

### Why are there no exact damage or health numbers?

The reviewed evidence does not establish dependable base values, and 1.0.9 difficulty modifies creature health and damage. Inventing a threshold would be less useful than explaining the movement pattern.

### Is the fight over when Pufferfish dies?

Combat is over, but story progression still requires the distinct drop and the tourist return.
