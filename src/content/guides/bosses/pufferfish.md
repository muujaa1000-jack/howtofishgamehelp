## Quick steps

1. Complete the tourist’s endangered-fish request and keep the Carrot reward.
2. Unlock the grill, cook several healing items, and prepare a steady ranged weapon.
3. Choose open ground away from trees before equipping the Carrot.
4. Sidestep each rolling approach, then shoot during the recovery gap.
5. In the later phase, circle around the purple trail instead of crossing it.
6. Pick up the boss drop and return it to the tourist for new coordinates.

## Win with spacing, not a damage race

Pufferfish turns terrain into part of the fight. Trees and props can stop a sideways dodge, while backpedaling keeps you on the boss’s attack line. Start in open ground and treat every roll as a lane: leave that lane, turn, deal measured damage, then move again.

The focused source describes a second phase with a purple poison trail and increasing body size. The safe distance from the first minute becomes unsafe later. Do not chase a perfect shot through the trail. Circle outside it, heal after any hit, and accept a longer fight rather than taking environmental damage.

## Why it may not work

- **The Carrot is missing:** complete the tourist hand-in and check that the catch satisfied the endangered condition.
- **The boss clips every dodge:** leave more lateral room and avoid fighting among coconut trees.
- **You die during healing:** eat after creating distance, not while the boss is already rolling toward you.
- **The purple trail cuts off your route:** go around its end instead of crossing directly behind the boss.
- **No next coordinates:** return the distinct Pufferfish drop to the tourist.

## Solo and co-op strategy

Solo is practical with disciplined movement and enough cooked fish. In co-op, spread into a shallow arc so the roll targets one lane and the other players keep safe firing angles. Everyone should watch the poison trail; reviving inside it can produce a second knockdown.
