## Direct answer

The source-supported story order is Lighthouse, forest island, desert island, rock island, then volcano. Every location is a dependency chain: speak to the local quest giver, satisfy the named request, preserve the special bait or result, defeat the required creature, collect its distinct drop, and complete the return conversation. At the volcano, the route continues after the first whale and after the mutated whale kill; the final drop, RHIB key, and starting the RHIB are all part of reaching the ending.

## Applies to

This walkthrough records the launch progression described by two independent guides and reviewed through the official 1.0.9 announcement. Patch 1.0.9 did not announce a story-order change, but its difficulty system can change boss health and damage relative to earlier descriptions. The route is source-based and has not been independently playtested by this site. Named conditions that the official patch does not address remain dependent on the cited walkthrough evidence.

## Quick steps

1. Finish the lighthouse keeper’s beer request, use the Empty Beer Can, defeat Spider Crab, and return its drop.
2. Take the boat keys and radar, then follow the route to the forest island.
3. Activate the forest request, collect three leeches, defeat Giant Piranha, and complete the hand-in.
4. On the desert island, unlock the grill, satisfy the tourist’s endangered-catch request, and use the Carrot for Pufferfish.
5. Return the Pufferfish drop to receive the next route.
6. On the rock island, obtain the required lure, catch Tuna, and use the Tuna to trigger the terrorizing bird encounter.
7. At the volcano, work through the scientist’s requests, the regular whale, and the mutated whale.
8. Return the final trophy, take the RHIB key, and start the RHIB to complete the route.

## Why this route works

The islands are not independent sandboxes even though each supports fishing and exploration. Progress is issued through conversations and hand-ins. Coordinates are usually a reward for proving that the local objective is complete. Sailing toward a known landmark before that state is recorded does not substitute for the missing trophy or dialogue.

The repeated pattern is helpful, but each island changes one part. The lighthouse teaches quest bait and boss drops. The forest begins with ground pickups. The desert links a catch condition to a Carrot reward and introduces the grill as practical preparation. The rock island turns a Tuna into encounter bait. The volcano chains more than one major fight and adds a final vehicle action. Remember the stable pattern—request, item, target, drop, return—while checking the local variation.

## Detailed walkthrough by island

### Lighthouse and Spider Crab

Speak to the keeper before spending heavily. Build a small cash buffer, prepare an early melee option, complete the beer request, and keep the Empty Beer Can. After Spider Crab falls, collect its unique item and return it. Do not depart with only the keys; take the radar as well.

### Forest and Giant Piranha

Find and activate the island’s request before assuming collected items count. The cited walkthroughs describe gathering three leeches, then fighting Giant Piranha and returning its drop. If leeches appear absent, use the focused [leech spawn guide](/fixes/leeches-not-spawning/) instead of roaming indefinitely.

### Desert and Pufferfish

Unlock the grill and speak to the tourist. The documented gate asks for an endangered catch and rewards a Carrot. Prepare cooked healing before equipping that boss bait. Fight Pufferfish in open space, collect its drop, and bring it back to the tourist. Patch 1.0.9 difficulty changes mean an old combat-time estimate would not be dependable, so this walkthrough gives no damage or health thresholds.

### Rock island and the terrorizing bird

The next dependency is a Tuna. Use the focused route to [catch and beat the Tuna boss](/bosses/tuna/), then keep its body for the aerial encounter rather than selling it. After the terrorizing bird is defeated, preserve its drop and finish the island hand-in before following later coordinates.

### Volcano and ending

Treat the volcano as a chain, not a single-boss arena. Follow the scientist’s instructions, handle the regular whale objective, then continue to the mutated whale encounter. The final kill alone is not the ending trigger described by the walkthroughs. Return its drop, receive the RHIB key, enter the correct boat, and start it.

## Common mistakes

- Collecting quest items before speaking to the local NPC, then assuming the counter recorded them.
- Selling a Tuna or discarding a distinctive boss drop because it looks like ordinary loot.
- Leaving an island after a kill without completing the return dialogue.
- Following coordinates without first collecting the travel item that makes them usable.
- Stopping after the first volcano whale or the final kill instead of completing the RHIB sequence.
- Treating Normal-mode combat advice as an exact promise for Easy or Hard.

## Why it may not work

If new coordinates do not appear, revisit the current island’s quest giver with the relevant item in inventory. If a boss does not trigger, verify the exact quest bait and confirm the request is active. If co-op progression seems split, check which player carries the item and whether the host recorded the hand-in. A crash, relay indicator, or damaged-save symptom is a separate technical problem; do not repeatedly consume progression items while the session is unstable.

## Safe recovery

When progression stalls, stop and reconstruct the last completed dependency. Record the active objective, inspect every party inventory, return to the current NPC, and restart only after preserving the save. Do not delete local or cloud data to fix a missing dialogue state. For a connection failure, use the [Steam relay guide](/fixes/steam-relay-connection-failed/). For loading or weapon-equip crashes, use the [save recovery guide](/fixes/save-file-corrupted-or-weapon-crash/).

## Solo and co-op differences

The documented island order is the same. Solo play makes item ownership obvious but gives no backup during boss triggers, so prepare before using special bait. Co-op can reduce combat pressure, yet it adds ambiguity: one player may collect the drop while another speaks to the NPC. Assign a single trophy carrier, keep the host present for the return, and confirm the next coordinates before anyone disconnects.

## Patch history and limitations

This page preserves its original 1.0.5 route label because that is the evidence range of the walkthrough sequence. The official 1.0.9 note adds difficulty settings and several troubleshooting changes but does not state that island gates or rewards changed. Easy reduces creature health and damage, Normal keeps the prior balance, and Hard increases them. Those options can affect preparation, not the documented order. Undocumented changes remain unknown until reliable evidence appears.

## FAQ

### Can I visit islands out of order?

Exploration may reveal locations, but the source-supported story rewards depend on completing each request and hand-in. This guide does not claim that sequence-breaking records progression safely.

### Why did a boss kill not unlock the next route?

The usual missing step is its unique drop or the return conversation. Check both before repeating the fight.

### Does difficulty change quests?

The 1.0.9 announcement documents health and damage modifiers, not alternate quests. Quest changes are therefore not claimed.

### When is the story actually complete?

The cited walkthroughs continue through returning the final mutated-whale drop, receiving the RHIB key, and starting that boat.
