## Quick steps

1. Clear the lighthouse keeper’s beer quest and defeat Spider Crab for the first boat keys.
2. On the forest island, collect three leeches, fight Giant Piranha, and return its drop.
3. On the desert island, unlock the grill, satisfy the tourist’s request, and use the Carrot for Pufferfish.
4. On the rock island, catch Tuna with the required lure and use it to draw the terrorizing bird.
5. At the volcano, help the scientist, defeat the regular whale, and trigger the mutated whale fight.
6. Return the final drop, take the RHIB key, and start that boat to reach the ending.

## The route is a chain of hand-ins

Both launch walkthroughs describe the same five-location order. The useful mental model is not “explore until something happens.” It is “find the local request, identify the bait or target it names, then return the recognizable result.” Coordinates are rewards, so sailing early cannot replace an unfinished conversation.

The forest and desert sections are easy to confuse because both ask you to bring an item to an NPC before the boss. Forest progression starts with ground pickups; desert progression starts with an endangered catch for the tourist. The rock island changes the pattern by making a Tuna the bait for an aerial encounter. The volcano then links two whale fights rather than ending after the first one.

## Why it may not work

- **No new coordinates:** revisit the island quest giver with the boss trophy equipped or in your inventory.
- **A boss will not trigger:** verify that you are using the quest-specific bait, not a similarly named purchasable lure.
- **You reached a location but cannot advance:** speak to the visible NPC before farming. Several counters begin only after the request is active.
- **The credits did not play:** the final boss drop must go back to the scientist, and the RHIB still has to be started.

## Solo and co-op notes

The quest order is shared by solo and co-op play. Co-op makes combat faster, but it also makes item custody less obvious. Choose one player to carry each unique trophy and confirm the hand-in before anyone travels. In solo, keep healing items ready before starting the later encounters because a failed summon costs more time than early fishing.
