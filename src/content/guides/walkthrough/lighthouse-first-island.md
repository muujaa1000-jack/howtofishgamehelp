## Quick steps

1. Talk to the lighthouse keeper and complete the opening food interaction.
2. Buy the basic rod, catch easy creatures, and sell enough to fund a melee weapon.
3. Improve that weapon at the nearby anvil instead of buying every early item.
4. Buy the beer requested by the keeper and give it to him.
5. Equip the returned Empty Beer Can as bait and fight Spider Crab.
6. Recover the crab’s drop, finish the hand-in, then take the boat key and radar.

## Build only for the current gate

The lighthouse economy is intentionally small. Easy catches fund a weapon; a better weapon makes the first quest encounter manageable. Two gameplay walkthroughs recommend a sharpened knife as the straightforward route, while some guides mention brass knuckles. The evidence does not support one universal “best” purchase across every player, so the durable advice is to improve one melee option rather than splitting money across both.

Do not use the quest bait the moment it appears. Clear the nearby space, make sure the weapon is in your inventory, and leave room for the boss drop. Spider Crab teaches an important pattern used throughout the story: the encounter and the quest completion are separate steps.

## Why it may not work

- **The keeper does not return usable bait:** make sure the beer was handed to the NPC involved in the active request.
- **Ordinary creatures keep biting:** equip the Empty Beer Can, not a normal lure or an unopened item.
- **Spider Crab escapes:** stay close enough to maintain pressure and attack after a missed charge.
- **No boat reward:** pick up the unique crab remains and complete the keeper’s dialogue.
- **You sail but cannot locate the route:** obtain the radar and use the coordinates awarded by progression.

## Solo and co-op notes

Solo players should enter with health and weapon preparation complete. In co-op, have one player bait the charge while others wait off its line, then collapse during the recovery. Decide who holds the drop before anyone reorganizes inventory.
