## Quick steps

1. Finish the lighthouse keeper’s beer request.
2. Defeat Spider Crab and collect the distinct item it leaves.
3. Return that item to the keeper and finish the conversation.
4. Confirm the boat keys were awarded and the boat can be operated.
5. Obtain the radar, board the boat, and follow the new route rather than open water guesses.
6. On arrival, speak with the forest NPC before searching for quest materials.

## What actually unlocks travel

There are three different pieces that players often collapse into one. The boss kill creates the hand-in item. The keeper’s reward enables the boat. The radar and coordinates tell you where progression continues. If any one of those is missing, “I beat the first boss” is not enough to diagnose the block.

The official 1.0.5 notes say a bug that allowed travel to any island without unlocking it was fixed. This matters because some release-week clips may show routes that no longer represent intended progression. The safe route is the NPC chain, and it also preserves the story rewards you will need later.

## Why it may not work

- **The boat cannot start:** verify the boss trophy was returned and the keys were actually received.
- **The radar has no useful direction:** finish the quest dialogue and confirm new coordinates were awarded.
- **You lost the boss drop:** return to the encounter area and check the ground before consuming more bait. Do not assume ordinary meat can replace it.
- **An old shortcut no longer works:** update to 1.0.5 and use the intended unlock chain.
- **You reached land but no objective advances:** locate and speak to the island quest giver first.

## Solo and co-op notes

In co-op, use the host’s active quest state as the reference and complete the hand-in before the session changes. If different players picked up the key item and activated the boat, regroup at the keeper. Solo players can pause and check inventory safely before departing.
