## Quick steps

1. Speak with the lighthouse keeper and pay attention to the current objective.
2. Catch low-risk creatures with the basic rod and free or cheap lure.
3. Sell catches, then buy a melee weapon and improve its damage at the anvil.
4. Complete the keeper’s beer request to receive the Empty Beer Can bait.
5. Defeat Spider Crab, pick up its distinct drop, and return it to the keeper.
6. Take the boat keys and obtain the radar before following the next coordinates.

## Understand the opening loop

The official Steam description is unusually honest about the structure: fish, kill, sell, buy better gear, complete quests, and beat bosses to reach new islands. Treat those as one loop, not six separate activities. A catch is useful because it funds the next tool; the tool is useful because it clears a quest; the quest produces the boss trigger; the boss drop unlocks travel.

Do not spend the opening trying to complete every collection entry. The first island exists to teach the economy and the hand-in pattern. Keep enough inventory space for quest items, and look at the object a boss leaves behind before you walk away.

## Why it may not work

- **The boss never appears:** normal bait does not replace the quest bait. Finish the keeper’s beer interaction first.
- **The fight takes too long:** the walkthrough evidence recommends improving a knife or another early melee choice before summoning the boss.
- **The quest does not finish:** defeating the target is not the final step. Recover its drop and return to the quest giver.
- **The next island is not visible:** the boat key unlocks travel, while the radar helps you follow the route. Check that you completed both parts.

## Solo and co-op notes

The game supports solo and online co-op. Progression items still matter in either mode. In co-op, agree on who carries the boss drop so it is not left behind during inventory shuffling. Solo players should prepare before using one-use quest bait because there is no teammate to revive or distract the boss.
