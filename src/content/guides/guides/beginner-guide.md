## Direct answer

Treat the first island as one connected route: accept the lighthouse keeper’s request, use ordinary catches to build a small cash reserve, buy only the equipment needed for that request, obtain the Empty Beer Can bait, defeat Spider Crab, and return its drop. The boat keys open travel, but the radar is what makes the next coordinates useful. The focused [boat keys and Island 2 unlock guide](/guides/unlock-next-island/) covers that final handoff. Leaving before both rewards are secured is the most avoidable early mistake.

## Applies to

This is a source-based route for the Steam release. The quest order was documented against version 1.0.5 and its sources were reviewed again after the official 1.0.9 announcement on August 25, 2026. Patch 1.0.9 added Easy, Normal, and Hard difficulty settings; it did not announce a change to the lighthouse quest chain. Combat pressure can therefore differ from older walkthrough descriptions even though the required hand-ins remain the same. No first-hand playtest is claimed here.

## Quick steps

1. Speak with the lighthouse keeper and note the active request.
2. Catch low-risk creatures with the starter equipment instead of buying several upgrades immediately.
3. Sell enough catches for a practical melee weapon and one useful improvement at the anvil.
4. Complete the keeper’s beer request and keep the Empty Beer Can bait.
5. Prepare before equipping the quest bait; it is the trigger, not an ordinary fishing supply.
6. Defeat Spider Crab, collect the distinct item it leaves, and take that item back to the keeper.
7. Collect the boat keys and the radar, then follow the coordinates rather than sailing at random.

## Why the opening route works

The official Steam description presents fishing, selling, buying equipment, completing quests, and defeating bosses as the game’s core loop. The opening becomes easier to understand when every purchase is judged by the next gate. A catch creates money; money buys a tool; the tool helps finish the request; the request provides the boss trigger; the boss drop completes the hand-in; the hand-in unlocks travel.

That structure also explains why collection cleanup is a poor first objective. A rare-looking catch may be worth keeping later, but the opening route does not require a complete Fishipedia. Keep room for named quest items and boss drops. If an object appears after a boss, inspect and collect it before moving away. Progress normally depends on the return conversation, not on the kill alone.

## Detailed first-island walkthrough

Begin at the lighthouse and exhaust the keeper’s useful dialogue. Do a short fishing-and-selling cycle close to safety. The aim is a buffer, not a fortune. Avoid buying multiple lures that solve no current objective. The launch walkthrough evidence recommends preparing a sharpened knife or another early melee option before the first boss; the exact shop path matters less than having a dependable close-range answer and enough resources to recover from mistakes.

When the beer request produces the Empty Beer Can, pause before using it. Check inventory space, healing supplies, and the area around the intended fight. Quest bait should be treated as a progression key. Equipping it without preparation can turn a simple gate into a repeat trip.

During Spider Crab, prioritize readable movement over constant attacking. Do not let the excitement of the first boss pull you away from the actual objective: the trophy and its hand-in. Once the fight ends, collect the unique drop, return to the keeper, and complete the conversation. Then verify that you have both travel pieces. The keys give access to the boat; the radar supports navigation to the next marked destination.

## Common mistakes

- **Using ordinary bait and waiting for the boss.** The quest reward is the relevant trigger.
- **Spending the first income on broad upgrades.** Buy for the active gate and preserve a recovery buffer.
- **Walking away after the kill.** The boss drop and the NPC return are part of the quest.
- **Treating coordinates as automatic travel.** Take the radar before leaving.
- **Following an older combat description as an exact difficulty promise.** Version 1.0.9 can alter health and damage through the selected difficulty.

## Why it may not work

If Spider Crab does not appear, return to the keeper and confirm the beer request is complete, then check that the Empty Beer Can—not a similarly ordinary lure—is equipped. If the keeper does not advance the quest, confirm the distinct boss drop is still in the party’s possession. If the next destination remains unclear, check the hand-in, keys, and radar separately; owning one does not prove the other two steps completed.

## Safe recovery

Do not delete a save because a trigger seems late. Move away, re-check the active objective, return to the quest giver, and restart the game only after preserving the current save. If the boss was defeated in co-op, ask every player to check inventory before repeating the encounter. For a crash or damaged-save symptom, stop experimenting with destructive file changes and use the dedicated [save and weapon crash guide](/fixes/save-file-corrupted-or-weapon-crash/).

## Solo and co-op differences

Solo players should prepare healing and equipment before consuming a quest trigger because no teammate can distract the target or hold a dropped item. In co-op, designate one person as the quest-item carrier. Everyone can contribute to combat, but only one clear custody chain is needed: trigger, trophy, hand-in. Confirm the return conversation before the host closes the lobby or the party sails.

## Patch history and limitations

The route evidence comes from the launch-era Steam description and an independent progression walkthrough. Patch 1.0.9 officially introduced three difficulty choices. Its announcement specifies Easy as lower creature health and damage, Normal as the prior balance, and Hard as higher creature health and damage. It does not document different quest rewards. This guide therefore treats the quest order as unchanged while marking combat advice as patch-sensitive. See [difficulty settings](/guides/difficulty-settings/) before choosing a mode.

## FAQ

### Should I farm money before the first boss?

Build a modest buffer for the next required purchase and recovery. There is no source-backed need to complete a large money grind before the first hand-in.

### Do I need every catch on the first island?

No. Collection goals can be cleaned up later. Early progression depends on the keeper’s request, the special bait, the boss drop, and travel rewards.

### Can I sail as soon as I get the keys?

You can access the boat, but the documented route also calls for the radar. Collect it before following the next coordinates.

### Which difficulty should a new player choose?

Normal preserves the pre-1.0.9 balance according to the official patch note. Easy reduces creature pressure; Hard increases it. Difficulty is a preference, not evidence that the story steps change.
