## Quick steps

1. Stop after the kill and locate the Pufferfish’s unique drop.
2. Store it before cooking, selling, or reorganizing other items.
3. Return to the tourist who gave the endangered-fish request.
4. Complete the hand-in and wait for the next coordinates to register.
5. Refill healing supplies and check the boat radar before departure.
6. At the rocky island, speak to the local NPC before buying a boss lure.

## The hand-in is the real checkpoint

Both progression walkthroughs agree that the desert boss result must go back to the tourist. Community sources vary between calling the item a fin or tail, so this page avoids making the label more certain than the evidence. What matters operationally is that it is the distinct object left by Pufferfish, not ordinary cooked meat.

The next stage changes combat range. Island 4 asks you to catch Tuna and use it to draw a large bird, so arrive with a ranged-weapon plan and money rather than spending everything on the desert island. The radar upgrade also reduces the chance of sailing past the new route.

## Why it may not work

- **No coordinates appear:** the tourist conversation or item hand-in is incomplete.
- **The drop seems missing:** return to the fight area and scan before reloading or using another bait.
- **The radar still points to the old task:** finish the quest, leave the menu, and verify the new objective.
- **You reached the fourth island but nothing starts:** speak to the local NPC; Tuna is part of a quest chain, not a random spawn instruction.
- **An old route guide differs:** use the live coordinates, especially after the 1.0.5 travel fix.

## Solo and co-op notes

In co-op, do not let the trophy carrier disconnect before the tourist hand-in. Confirm the host’s objective updates, then travel together. Solo players can use the pause between islands to cook, sell spare catches, and keep the key drop isolated from ordinary inventory.

## Prepare for the selected difficulty

The travel and Tuna gate are not announced as changed, while later creatures use the Easy, Normal, or Hard modifiers added in 1.0.9. Keep the Pufferfish trophy return and next coordinates as separate checks, then review [difficulty settings](/guides/difficulty-settings/) before the next encounter instead of assuming older combat pacing.
