## Quick steps

1. Find the lady by the lake and activate her request before collecting.
2. Walk the forest floor slowly and watch for an interaction prompt in the grass.
3. Search around the island rather than repeatedly fishing the same water.
4. Stop when the objective shows all three leeches collected.
5. Hand them to the lady and take the bait she returns.
6. Equip that bait on the fishing rod to begin the Giant Piranha encounter.

## Search the ground, not the water

Two gameplay-backed guides agree on the core condition: the quest needs three leeches, and the forest NPC converts them into boss bait. The leeches are world pickups. This is why casting more often does not help and why players can mistake the task for a broken fishing objective.

Use the on-screen counter as the authoritative completion check. A route description can become stale after terrain adjustments, but a live counter tells you whether the current save registered the pickup. Move methodically around grass, shore edges, and the spaces between props; look for the prompt instead of expecting a large creature model.

## Why it may not work

- **The counter never started:** speak to the lady first and exhaust the quest dialogue.
- **Fishing catches normal creatures:** leeches are ground pickups for this request.
- **You found fewer than three:** make a full sweep, checking interaction prompts hidden by grass.
- **The pickups appear absent:** use the separate spawn-problem page; do not invent a respawn timer.
- **The boss will not bite:** return the three leeches for the converted quest bait, then equip that item.

## Solo and co-op notes

In co-op, have everyone report pickups against the host’s visible quest counter. Do not split into separate sessions during the collection. Once the converted bait is received, regroup and make sure the chosen carrier has a prepared shotgun and open space for the boss drop.
