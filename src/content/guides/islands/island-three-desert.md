## Quick steps

1. Speak to both the grill NPC and the tourist so their objectives are visible.
2. Use the island’s standard lure tier to catch the requested grill-side creature.
3. Finish that interaction and start the grill, giving you a reliable cooking option.
4. Catch a species the game flags as endangered and give it to the tourist.
5. Take the Carrot reward, stock cooked fish, and summon Pufferfish.
6. Return the Pufferfish drop to the tourist for the next coordinates.

## Run the two quests in a useful order

The grill quest is not the direct story gate, but completing it first makes the boss route safer. Cooked fish is used for healing during later encounters, and the desert is where the mechanic becomes practical. The tourist route is the progression gate: an endangered catch earns the Carrot that draws Pufferfish.

Do not confuse “endangered” with “Drip.” The source-backed tourist guide identifies endangered as an on-screen species flag, while Drip refers to rare variants used in collection goals. Follow the label shown on the catch and the tourist objective rather than judging appearance alone.

## Why it may not work

- **The tourist refuses the fish:** verify the catch was marked endangered and that his quest was active first.
- **You found a colorful variant:** a Drip appearance does not automatically replace the required species condition.
- **No Carrot appears:** finish the hand-in dialogue and check inventory before catching another fish.
- **Pufferfish does not trigger:** equip the Carrot as the boss bait on the rod.
- **The next route stays locked:** recover the Pufferfish drop and return it to the tourist.

## Solo and co-op notes

Solo players benefit from unlocking the grill before the boss and carrying several cooked items. In co-op, assign one player to keep Pufferfish moving through open space while the others shoot from different angles. Keep everyone out of the same poison path.
