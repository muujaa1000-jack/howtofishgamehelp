## Quick steps

1. Speak to the volcano scientist and activate his five-catch request.
2. Use the Scientific Lure route and hand in five valid local catches.
3. Prepare an upgraded assault rifle, open inventory space, and a large healing supply.
4. Use the scientist’s whale bait and defeat the regular whale.
5. Carry the whale up the wooden route and throw it into the volcano.
6. Defeat the mutated whale, return its drop, receive the RHIB key, and start the boat.

## Prepare for two fights, not one

The volcano sequence links a regular whale to the final mutated version. Do not spend all healing or ammunition assuming the first health bar is the finale. The focused whale evidence recommends a sustained-fire weapon with attachments and a large stack of cooked fish. The Destructoid route also confirms that the first carcass is a quest object: it has to reach the volcano to begin the last encounter.

After the mutated whale dies, the story still has two interactions. Take its distinct drop back to the scientist, then use the key he gives you on the RHIB. “We are so back” describes finishing the game, so stopping at the boss arena leaves the actual ending untriggered.

## Why it may not work

- **The scientist’s counter does not increase:** use the requested local catch route and hand items directly to him.
- **No whale bait appears:** complete all five registered hand-ins and finish the dialogue.
- **Throwing the carcass does nothing:** follow the visible plank route to the intended volcano point rather than dropping it elsewhere.
- **The final fight overwhelms you:** prepare before the first whale; the encounters are connected.
- **No credits or completion achievement:** return the final drop, collect the RHIB key, and start the RHIB.

## Solo and co-op notes

Solo players should stage healing near a safe, memorable point before activating the chain. In co-op, split fire while preserving room to dodge; revives help, but clustered players can all be hit by one area attack. Keep the final trophy with the host until the scientist hand-in finishes.

## Difficulty and save boundary

The whale encounters use the current difficulty’s creature health and damage modifiers, while no alternate ending condition is announced. Preserve the final trophy and complete the scientist return before experimenting. If this save fails while loading or crashes on weapon equip, stop repeating the ending and follow the [preservation-first crash guide](/fixes/save-file-corrupted-or-weapon-crash/).
