## Quick steps

1. Finish the lighthouse quest for boat access and the first route.
2. Bring three leeches to the forest NPC, then return the Giant Piranha trophy.
3. Help the desert tourist, use the Carrot bait, and return the Pufferfish drop.
4. Prepare Tuna on the rock island, defeat the terrorizing bird, and return its head.
5. Complete the scientist’s volcano request, then survive both whale encounters.
6. Use the final key rather than assuming the last boss kill ends the game.

## Progression table

| Area | Local gate | Main encounter | What advances you |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lighthouse | Beer-thief request | Spider Crab | Boss drop returned for boat keys; radar guides travel |
| Forest | Three leeches | Giant Piranha | Distinct piranha drop returned for coordinates |
| Desert | Tourist’s endangered catch | Pufferfish | Pufferfish drop returned for the next route |
| Rocks | Tuna prepared as bait | Terrorizing bird | Bird trophy returned for volcano coordinates |
| Volcano | Scientist’s fish request | Whale, then mutated whale | Final drop returned for RHIB access |

Sources consistently use descriptive labels—forest, desert, rocks, volcano—and numeric shorthand. If the in-game objective uses a different label after a patch, follow the objective rather than an old guide heading.

## New mini islands and the main route

The [official Islet Update](https://steamcommunity.com/games/4001890/announcements/detail/717916455762395911) added multiple mini islands and adjusted distances between islands. This table tracks the older five-location story chain; it does not list every place you can see or visit after the update. The announcement does not specify a quest order or exact coordinates for the new mini islands.

Patch 1.1.2 enlarged the [mini-island dots on the radar](/items/radar-guide/). If you see an extra dot while travelling, check whether your active objective names a story destination before treating that dot as the next required hand-in.

## Why it may not work

- **Radar shows nothing useful:** make sure the previous boss item was returned and the new coordinates were actually awarded.
- **You can visit an island but its boss is absent:** location access and boss activation are separate; start the local NPC request.
- **Your objective remains on the old island:** return to the correct quest giver with the unique trophy, not ordinary meat.
- **A guide calls an area by another name:** community labels vary. Match its terrain, NPC, and objective instead of relying on the nickname alone.
- **The radar shows more islands than the table:** the 1.1.0 update added mini islands; this table covers the main story chain only.

## Solo and co-op notes

Island order does not change with party size. Patch 1.0.5 also closed an unintended route that let players travel to locked islands, so older shortcut advice should not be treated as the normal path. In co-op, keep the host’s active objective visible and do the hand-in before changing sessions.
