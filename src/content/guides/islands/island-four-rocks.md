## Quick steps

1. Activate the island NPC’s large-bird request before buying the boss lure.
2. Prepare a ranged weapon and healing, then acquire the Professional Boss Lure.
3. Fish up Tuna and avoid its jumping approach while dealing damage.
4. Pick up the defeated Tuna and place it on the ground as bird bait.
5. Move under solid cover and shoot the large bird between its passes.
6. Return the bird’s distinct trophy for the volcano coordinates.

## This island has a two-target chain

[Tuna is the bait-producing mini-boss](/bosses/tuna/), not the story boss. That distinction prevents two common mistakes: eating or selling the Tuna, and leaving after the Tuna kill as if the island were complete.

Both gameplay sources recommend ranged fire for the bird. The Destructoid walkthrough describes an Uzi or sniper-style approach and warns against treating a short-range shotgun as the ideal choice. The focused boss guide adds a useful positional rule: use a building to break the dive or ranged attack, then expose yourself only during the recovery.

## Why it may not work

- **Tuna will not appear:** use the correct Professional Boss Lure after the quest is active.
- **The bird never arrives:** place the Tuna on the ground; do not keep it only in inventory.
- **You take damage without seeing the target:** move under cover and track the approach marker or audio cue.
- **The NPC gives no coordinates:** recover the bird’s unique trophy and complete the return dialogue.
- **You used the Tuna for food:** catch another only after confirming the quest still needs the bait.

## Solo and co-op notes

Solo players should choose cover with a clear exit and avoid chasing the bird across open terrain. In co-op, spread around one building so a single pass does not hit everyone. One player can call the approach while others reload under cover; name the trophy carrier after the kill.
