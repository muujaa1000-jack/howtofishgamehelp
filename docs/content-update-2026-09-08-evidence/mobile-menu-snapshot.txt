            - paragraph [ref=f57e59]: How to Fish added Steam Cloud in 1.0.12. On the device that already holds your working save, update the game, play once, and exit normally to trigger its first upload. Leave Steam online and wait for synchronization to finish before opening the game on your PC or Steam Deck. Stop if Steam reports a conflict or failed sync; do not overwrite the only copy.             - heading "Quick steps" [level=2] [ref=f57e61]
            - list [ref=f57e62]:
              - listitem [ref=f57e63]: Start on the device containing the working progress you want to keep. Let Steam install 1.0.12 or a later available update.
              - listitem [ref=f57e64]: Check that Steam Cloud is enabled for the game and Steam is online. Use the same Steam account on the other device.
              - listitem [ref=f57e65]: Play How to Fish once on the original device, then quit normally. This is the first-upload step specified by the developer.
              - listitem [ref=f57e66]: Keep Steam running and wait until the game’s Cloud status shows synchronization has finished. Do not launch the other copy while upload is pending.
              - listitem [ref=f57e67]: On the destination PC or Steam Deck, let Steam finish synchronization before starting the game.
              - listitem [ref=f57e68]: Check that the expected save and progress are present before continuing. If progress is missing or Steam shows a warning, stop and investigate that state first.
            - heading "Why an older save may not appear yet" [level=2] [ref=f57e69]

              - text: The

              - text: adds Cloud storage and explicitly asks players to run and exit the game on the device holding their existing save. Installing the update on a second device alone does not complete that first-upload step.
            - paragraph [ref=f57e72]: The developer’s instruction establishes how to start the upload. Waiting for Steam’s synchronization status and checking the destination save are platform checks and editorial completion checks. This site has not performed a PC-to-Deck or Deck-to-PC transfer in-game.
            - heading "If Steam reports a conflict or failed synchronization" [level=2] [ref=f57e73]
            - paragraph [ref=f57e74]:
              - text: Do not continue a new play session on the other device while synchronization is unresolved. Check that the original device is online, the game has fully closed, and its upload has completed. Follow

                - /url: https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/68D2-35AB-09A9-7678

            - paragraph [ref=f57e76]: If local and cloud copies conflict, identify which device contains the progress you intend to keep and preserve the available copies before choosing a replacement. Do not assume that a cloud copy is always the correct one, or approve an overwrite when you cannot identify the copies. This guide provides no unverified save path or manual file-replacement recipe.

            - heading "Synchronization is different from corruption recovery" [level=2] [ref=f57e88]
            - paragraph [ref=f57e89]: Patch 1.0.11 introduced save checks and backups. Patch 1.0.12 adds Cloud synchronization. The latter can move a save between devices, but neither announcement establishes that syncing reconstructs damaged progress. A synchronized copy can still contain a failing save. Preserve it rather than deleting it or replacing it with an empty run.
            - heading "Solo and co-op saves" [level=2] [ref=f57e90]
            - paragraph [ref=f57e91]: Use the device that holds the save you actually want to continue. Being a guest in another player’s lobby does not establish that your device owns that session’s progression. The 1.0.12 note announces Cloud storage, not a transfer of host ownership or a way to merge several players’ saves. For a co-op discrepancy, record the host and joiner roles before changing files.
            - heading "FAQ" [level=2] [ref=f57e92]
            - heading "Does How to Fish support Steam Cloud now?" [level=3] [ref=f57e93]
            - paragraph [ref=f57e94]: Yes, according to the official 1.0.12 announcement reviewed September 8, 2026. Existing saves need the original-device play-and-exit step for their first upload.
            - heading "Can I close the game and immediately switch to Steam Deck?" [level=3] [ref=f57e95]
            - paragraph [ref=f57e96]: Wait until Steam finishes synchronization first. Then allow the destination device to sync before launching and confirm the expected progress.
            - heading "Will Cloud fix a corrupted save?" [level=3] [ref=f57e97]
            - paragraph [ref=f57e98]: Cloud synchronization is not a documented corruption-repair procedure. Stop at a load failure or unresolved conflict and preserve the copies you have.
